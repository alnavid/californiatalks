SMS Consent & Opt-In

Receive California Talks survey invitations by text message.

California Talks runs short, anonymous public-opinion surveys for campaigns, government agencies, and ballot measure teams. If you would like to participate, you can opt in below to receive survey invitations by SMS from our toll-free number.

What you are agreeing to

By submitting the form on this page and checking the consent box, you give California Talks your express written consent to send you SMS text messages inviting you to take part in opinion-research surveys.

Program description

  • Sender: California Talks (californiatalks.org)
  • Message type: Survey invitations and, when needed, one short reminder per survey
  • Message frequency: Variable, typically 0 to 2 messages per month
  • Message and data rates may apply (charged by your mobile carrier)
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages

How to opt out

You can cancel the SMS service at any time. Reply STOP to any message we send you and you will be unsubscribed. After you send STOP, we will send you one confirmation message and will not send any further texts unless you opt in again.

How to get help

For help, reply HELP to any message we send, or email ali@californiatalks.org. We will respond with a short message describing the program and how to opt out.

Sample message

From California Talks California Talks: Your opinion matters. Take our 3-min district survey: https://californiatalks.org/s/abc Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg&data rates may apply.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories of information described in our Privacy Policy exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

California Talks does not sell, rent, or share your phone number or personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Phone numbers collected through this opt-in form are used only to deliver the survey invitations you have consented to receive. We retain your number only for the duration of your participation, and we delete it on request. To request deletion, email ali@californiatalks.org from the email address on file, or reply STOP to any survey invitation. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Terms

This SMS program is operated by California Talks for non-commercial public-opinion research. You must be the subscriber of the mobile number you enter, or be authorized to opt in on its behalf. California Talks may end or modify the program at any time without notice. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
