Privacy Policy

How California Talks collects, uses, and protects your information.

California Talks conducts public-opinion research for campaigns, government agencies, and ballot measure teams. We are committed to protecting the privacy of the individuals who interact with us through our website, our surveys, and our SMS opt-in program.

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Who we are

California Talks (“California Talks,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is a public-opinion research firm based in California. You can reach us by email at ali@californiatalks.org or through the contact form at californiatalks.org.

2. Information we collect

We collect a limited set of information so that we can operate our research programs:

  • Contact-form submissions. When you submit our project-intake form, we collect your name, email address, project type, geography, and the description you provide.
  • Newsletter subscriptions. When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address, optional first name, signup source, confirmation timestamp, and the version of this Privacy Policy in effect when you confirmed.
  • SMS opt-in submissions. When you opt in through our SMS Consent & Opt-In page, we collect your name, mobile phone number, and (optionally) your email address and ZIP code, along with a timestamped record of your consent.
  • Survey responses. When you take part in one of our surveys, we collect the answers you provide. Survey responses are processed in aggregate for research purposes.
  • Publicly available voter file data. For some research projects we use phone numbers, addresses, and demographic indicators obtained from publicly available voter registration files. This data is used solely for non-commercial public-opinion research.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting provider may log standard request information (IP address, user agent, timestamp) for security and reliability purposes.

3. How we use information

We use the information described above only for the following purposes:

  • To respond to inquiries you send us through the contact form.
  • To send the California Talks newsletter to people who confirm their subscription.
  • To deliver the SMS survey invitations you have consented to receive.
  • To conduct, analyze, and report on public-opinion research projects.
  • To honor opt-out, deletion, and other privacy requests.
  • To comply with applicable law and protect the security of our systems.

We do not use survey responses, SMS opt-in information, or newsletter information for third-party advertising, and we do not build advertising profiles about you.

4. SMS messaging and mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All categories of information described in this Privacy Policy exclude text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

When you opt in to receive SMS survey invitations from California Talks, your phone number and consent record are used exclusively to send the survey invitations and related operational messages (for example, the confirmation message you receive after replying STOP).

Message frequency is variable and is typically 0–2 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply, charged by your mobile carrier. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message we send. You can request help by replying HELP or by emailing ali@californiatalks.org.

Full program details, the sample message, and the opt-in form are available on our SMS Consent & Opt-In page.

5. How we share information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes. We share information only in the limited situations below:

  • Service providers. We use a small number of vetted service providers to operate our research and messaging programs (for example, our website host, our form-submission processor, email delivery provider, survey platform, and SMS messaging carrier). These providers process data only on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services.
  • Clients of our research. We deliver aggregated, de-identified survey results to the campaigns, agencies, or ballot-measure teams who commission a given research project. Individual respondents are not identified to our clients.
  • Legal compliance. We may disclose information when required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of California Talks or others.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected. Contact-form submissions are retained for the duration of the related business inquiry. SMS opt-in records and phone numbers are retained for the duration of your participation in the program and are deleted on request. Newsletter contact and consent records are retained while you remain subscribed and as reasonably necessary to honor unsubscribe requests. Survey response data is retained in aggregated form for research and historical purposes.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Unsubscribe from email. Use the unsubscribe link included in every newsletter or email ali@californiatalks.org.
  • Opt out of SMS. Reply STOP to any California Talks text message and we will permanently remove your number from our SMS contact list.
  • Access or delete your information. Email ali@californiatalks.org from the email or phone associated with your record and we will fulfill reasonable access or deletion requests.
  • California residents. If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we hold about you and the right to request its deletion. To exercise these rights, email ali@californiatalks.org.

8. Children

California Talks does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 16. Our research programs are directed at adult voters and residents. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us at ali@californiatalks.org and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the information we collect. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we work to limit access to personal information to authorized personnel and trusted service providers.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of the page indicates when the policy was most recently revised. Material changes will be posted on this page.

11. Contact us

Questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our handling of your information can be sent to ali@californiatalks.org. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
