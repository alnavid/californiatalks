Our founder

Ali Navid

Founder & President, California Talks LLC

Ali founded California Talks in 2018, bringing together his background in public service and experience conducting public opinion research at the local, state and federal levels.

His approach combines a practical understanding of California politics and government with careful survey design and clear analysis. Through California Talks, Ali helps organizations better understand public opinion and make decisions informed by research.
